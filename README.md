## ⬇️ Download & Setup (about 2 minutes)

1. **Download** the latest zip from the [**Releases page**](https://github.com/arjun408408/nalox-aura/releases/latest) and unzip it.
2. **Run `INSTALL.bat`** — keep After Effects **fully closed** while it installs.
3. **Run `SETUP.bat` once** — it installs the tools Aura needs (yt-dlp + ffmpeg), creates your folders, guides the one-time YouTube sign-in and checks that everything works.
4. Open After Effects → **Window → Extensions → Nalox Aura**. Done.

After that, updates arrive **inside the panel** — you never need to download again.

**Requirements:** Windows 10/11 • Adobe After Effects 2022 or newer.

## ✨ What's Inside

- **Fonts** — search and install fonts (DaFont + Google Fonts), plus **Font Finder**: show it a picture of a font and it finds the font for you.
- **Captions** — automatic captions from any video's audio, with karaoke styles.
- **55 Presets** — colour grading, text effects, glow & outline, shake & motion, atmosphere.
- **Templates & Text Animations** — ready-made templates, 37 text animations and graph presets with a live preview.
- **AI Clipping** 🔒 — give it a video; the AI understands the content and cuts the best moments into captioned 9:16 clips.
- **Live Clipping** 🔒 — paste a running live-stream link; Aura records it and clips the moments where the **live chat suddenly explodes** — streamer name on top, topic line, facecam panel, no blur.
- **Downloader** — bring videos straight into your project, in formats After Effects loves.
- **Nalox AI** 🔒 — describe the project, pick a materials folder, and watch it build the project inside After Effects for you.
- **Quick Tools** — tracking & stabilisation, shake studio, smooth slow motion, SFX, beat markers and more.
